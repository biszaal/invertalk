# Invertalk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [PUBLISH DATE]_

Invertalk is a party game for two people sharing one phone. It is made by [LEGAL NAME, e.g. Biszaal Tech] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your voice and with other information, in plain language.

**The short version:** your voice never leaves your phone unless you choose to share a recording. We don't ask for your name, email or any account. We collect a small amount of anonymous usage data to learn whether the game works and is fun. It never includes your voice or the words you choose.

## Your voice and the phrases you choose

- **Microphone.** Invertalk uses the microphone only while you are recording a phrase or an imitation. It asks for permission first and does not record in the background.
- **Processed on your phone.** Recording, reversing and playing audio all happen on your device. Your recordings are **never uploaded** to us or to anyone else.
- **Temporary.** Recordings are stored temporarily in the app's private storage. They are deleted when a game ends, when you retry or pick a new phrase, when you leave the game, and when the app next starts.
- **Phrases stay private.** The phrase you pick or type stays on your phone. It is never sent to us, never included in analytics, and never added to anything you share.
- **Sharing is your choice.** If you tap **Share the reveal**, your phone's share menu opens with that one audio clip. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

## Information stored only on your phone

- **Your recording consent.** When you agree to the in-app recording notice, the app saves the date you agreed, so it doesn't ask again. You can read the notice again any time in **Settings**.
- **Analytics identifier.** A random identifier created by the app (see below), and any analytics events waiting to be sent.

Deleting the app removes all of this from your phone.

## Anonymous usage analytics

We use **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc.) to understand how the game is used, for example whether people finish a challenge or share a result. This helps us fix problems and decide what to improve.

**What is sent:**

- **A random identifier** generated by the app. It is not your name, email, phone number or advertising ID, and it isn't linked to any account (there are no accounts).
- **These events:**
  - you saw, accepted or declined the recording notice;
  - you started a challenge (with the id of a built-in phrase such as `p03`, or just "custom" for your own phrase — never the words);
  - microphone permission was granted or denied;
  - a recording finished (its length, sample rate, channel count and audio format) or was interrupted (by a call, by leaving the app, or by an error);
  - reversing a recording succeeded or failed (how long it took, plus an error code);
  - a challenge reached the reveal (how long it took);
  - the share menu was opened and closed;
  - the app hit an unexpected error (a short error code and the name of the screen).
- **Basic app and device details** that PostHog adds automatically: app version, operating system and version, device manufacturer and model, language/region setting and time zone.

**What is never sent:** your voice or any audio, the phrase you chose, anything you type, your device's name, your contacts, or your precise or approximate location. We have switched off PostHog's session recording, automatic tap tracking and location lookup. We have also configured our PostHog project to discard IP addresses.

We do not use analytics for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent any data.

**Where and how long:** PostHog processes this data for us on servers in [the United States / the EU — match the PostHog host you use]. We keep analytics data for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 12 months] and then delete it.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Microphone**: to record the phrase and the imitation. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings. The game then can't record, but nothing else changes.
- **Internet**: only to send the anonymous analytics described above.

Invertalk does not use your camera, photos, contacts, location or files, and it shows no ads.

## Children

Invertalk is a general-audience party game with original, family-friendly phrases. It doesn't ask anyone for personal information, has no chat or accounts, and shows no ads. The only data that leaves the phone is the anonymous usage data described above.

## Your choices and rights

- **Stop recording:** turn off microphone access in your phone's settings.
- **Delete local data:** delete the app. This removes your consent record, the analytics identifier and any recordings still on the phone.
- **Analytics data:** because it is anonymous and not linked to you, we usually can't find your data on request. If you have a question or a request about your data, contact us at the address below and we'll help where we can.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Contact us to exercise them. For the anonymous analytics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, the app will ask for your agreement again before you next record.
