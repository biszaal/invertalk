# Invertalk privacy policy

The published policy is **https://www.biszaaltech.com/games/invertalk/privacy**.

Its source is `src/pages/InvertalkPrivacy.tsx` in the biszaal-tech-website repo; edit it there. This repo keeps no copy, so the two can't drift apart. The app links to the URL from `src/config.ts` (`PRIVACY_POLICY_URL`), in Settings and on the consent screen.

If the app's audio handling, analytics events, PostHog region or plan change, update that page before the build ships.
