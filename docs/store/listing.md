## Full description (App Store and Play)

```text
Invertalk is the party game where you talk backwards.

Player 1 picks a phrase and says it. Invertalk flips the recording so it plays backwards. Pass the phone to Player 2, who listens and copies the strange backwards sounds as closely as they can. Then Invertalk flips their attempt forwards, and everyone hears what they really said. Sometimes it's spot on. Usually it's hilarious.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Pick a starter phrase or type your own.
2. Player 1 records it and hears it played backwards.
3. Hand the phone over. No peeking at the phrase!
4. Player 2 copies the backwards sound.
5. The reveal: their attempt, flipped forwards.

Replay the reveal, compare it with the original, show the phrase, try again, or share the funniest reveal with friends.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Two players, one phone: made for parties, road trips and family nights
• Original, family-friendly starter phrases, or type your own
• No ads, no account, no in-app purchases
• No scores or judging, just the reveal
• Works without an internet connection

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your recordings are processed on your phone and are never uploaded. They're deleted when the game ends, and the phrase you choose never leaves your device. We collect only anonymous usage stats to improve the game, never your voice or your words.
```
